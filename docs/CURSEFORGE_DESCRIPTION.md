# Replication Extended

An unofficial Replication addon with eight permanently type-bound matter tanks, including when empty.

## Dedicated storage for every matter type

Create separate tanks for Earth, Nether, Organic, Ender, Metallic, Precious, Living, and Quantum matter. Each tank only accepts its assigned type, even after its contents have been completely consumed.

The tanks connect to Replication's matter network from above and below. They include matter-colored textures, a dynamic contents display, network priority settings, and optional Jade tooltips. Filled tanks retain stored matter when broken and placed again.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.250 or later in the 21.1 series.
- Replication and Titanium, together with their required dependencies.
- Install the addon on both client and server.
- Jade is optional.

This beta is built against Replication 1.21.1-1.2.7 and Titanium 1.21-4.0.42. Back up your world before testing.

## Crafting

Place an empty Replication matter tank in the center of the crafting grid with four matching catalysts above, below, left, and right:

- Earth: dirt
- Nether: netherrack
- Organic: bone meal
- Ender: ender pearl
- Metallic: iron ingot
- Precious: gold ingot
- Living: slime ball
- Quantum: amethyst shard

Empty the original tank before crafting; the current recipes do not transfer stored matter.
